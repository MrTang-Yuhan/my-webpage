## 001. 小镇英语老师经济独立，为何不混任何圈子

[全英｜小镇英语老师经济独立，为何不混任何圈子](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hyuy6WEpc/?spm_id_from=333.1007.top_right_bar_window_history.content.click&vd_source=e98b669ccbafff4b5aa59dd6303b722f)

### 中英文


I'm a young independent English teacher. At a young age, I've achieved financial independence and have complete control over my life and time.
 
I used to believe that social circles mean opportunities, resources and connections.
But after many years of growing and settling down, I finally came to realize the truth.
Most social circles don't help you grow. They just consume your precious time, drain your energy and disturb your inner peace.
As a small‑town teacher, my life is simple, but fulfilling.
I spend most of my time teaching, improving my professional skills, reading, and living at my own pace.
I choose to stay away from massive social circles, not because I'm arrogant, lonely or unsociable. It's because I have learned to screen my life.
I would rather devote my spare time to self‑growth than waste it on useless socializing.
I would rather enjoy peaceful solitude than force myself to blend in with the crowd that does not match my mindset.
Solitude gives me clarity of mind. It allows me to focus on what truly matters, to keep growing steadily, and to have a stable, independent, authentic life.
You don't need countless acquaintances to prove your worth.
A stable life, continuous self‑improvement and a peaceful heart are the best assets in life. I believe the best social circle you can ever have is yourself.

 
我是一名年轻的独立英语教师。年纪轻轻，我就实现了经济独立，能够完全掌控自己的生活与时间。
 
我曾经以为社交圈就代表机会、资源与人脉。
但经过多年的成长与沉淀，我终于明白了这个道理。
大多数社交圈并不会助你成长。它们只会消耗你宝贵的时间，耗尽你的精力，扰乱你内心的平静。
作为一名小镇教师，我的生活简单，却很充实。
我把大部分时间用来教学、提升专业能力、阅读，按照自己的节奏生活。
我远离繁杂的社交圈，并不是因为我傲慢、孤独或是不善交际，而是因为我学会过滤生活中的纷扰。
我宁愿把业余时间投入自我成长，也不愿把时间浪费在无意义的社交上。
我宁愿享受宁静的独处，也不愿强迫自己融入和我三观不合的人群。
独处带给我清醒的思绪。它让我专注于真正重要的事物，持续稳步成长，拥有稳定、独立、忠于本心的生活。
你不需要依靠无数泛泛之交来证明自身的价值。
安稳的生活、持续的自我提升以及一颗平和的心，是人生最宝贵的财富。我相信，你所能拥有的最好的社交圈，就是你自己。
